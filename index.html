Scratchpad

Capture the moment.

Notes that sit beside whatever you're working on, kept with each task.

Add to Chrome

Free. An early version for a small group of testers, so please don't share the link.

Getting started

  1. Click Add to Chrome, then pin Scratchpad: puzzle piece at the top right, then the pin.
  2. Answer the short setup. It fits Scratchpad to your kind of work.
  3. Go to any website and click the Scratchpad icon. Your notes open beside the page.
  4. Working on several things? Group the tabs for each one. Every group gets its own notes.

Things to try

  • Write a date like "Fri 2pm" next to a to-do. It can go straight to your calendar or reminders.
  • Mention a client or a person. Everything you've written about them collects in one place.
  • Click the tab on the edge of your notes to tuck them away, and again to bring them back.
